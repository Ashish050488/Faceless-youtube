# Spec or Wreck — verdict benchmark (owner 2026-11-18: "it is random now; decide on a benchmark")

Every video ends SPEC or WRECK from this score, never from feel. Score only from sourced facts (factsheet.md).
Write the score table into factsheet.md; say the verdict reason in the script; show "SPEC SCORE 72" on the end card.

## Score (100 points)
| # | Criterion | How to score | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Price vs rivals** | Official price vs the 3 closest current rivals with the same headline spec (official prices, same market). ≤ 10 % below their median = 25 · within ±10 % = 15 · 10–30 % above = 8 · > 30 % above = 0 | 25 |
| 2 | **Headline claim holds up** | Main marketed number vs an independent test or the brand's own fine print (test conditions). Real/claimed ≥ 0.9 = 20 · 0.75–0.9 = 12 · 0.5–0.75 = 6 · < 0.5 or contradicted = 0. No test yet → score the fine print and say "claimed". | 20 |
| 3 | **Genuinely new or best in class** | Measurably first/best in its class = 15 · matches the best = 8 · behind = 0 | 15 |
| 4 | **Lasts and can be fixed** | iFixit score if published (≥ 7 = 15 · 4–6 = 8 · ≤ 3 = 0). Else: replaceable battery +5, ≥ 5 yrs software/security updates +5, spare parts sold by the brand +5 | 15 |
| 5 | **The catch** | Worst downside: minor (accessory sold separately, one colour) = 15 · moderate (big claim needs fine print, missing common feature) = 8 · severe (core feature behind a subscription, official recall/safety notice, regulator privacy finding) = 0 | 15 |
| 6 | **Can people buy it** | On sale in US/EU/India now = 10 · official date announced = 5 · no date / one region only = 0 | 10 |

**Verdict:** ≥ 55 = **SPEC** (k snaps upright) · < 55 = **WRECK** (k falls). Always add who it is / isn't for
("Spec — if you travel a lot"). If a criterion has no data, give it half points and say so in the factsheet.
Re-check the cut-off after 20 videos (are verdicts balanced? the benchmark doesn't need 50/50, but 20/20 SPEC means
the scoring is too soft).

## Fairness rules (keeps us honest and safe)
- The verdict is our opinion from these published criteria; the script says "by our score". Facts stay sourced.
- Never call a product dangerous, fake or a scam unless an official recall/regulator/court source says so.
- Compare like with like (same class, same market, same currency, launch prices).

## Sensitive topics — skip, or cover without a verdict
- Skip: weapons, medical/diagnostic claims beyond regulator clearance (FDA/CE text only), products for children's
  safety, gambling, adult, political or tragedy-linked stories, leaks/rumours.
- Cover without a SPEC/WRECK verdict (facts only, end on "what it means"): recalls, safety incidents, layoffs/CEO news.
