# Brand — "Spec or Wreck" (current only; older palettes/logos are in git history)

Name **Spec or Wreck** · handle **@specorwreck** · tagline "Great spec... or a wreck?" · banner line "New tech. Straight verdicts."
Promise: new tech in under 2 minutes — what it is, the clever part, THE CATCH, the price, who should buy it.

## Palette v4 "Warm" — LOCKED
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | `#FAF8F5` | main flat background |
| lavender / blush | `#ECE8FB` / `#FBE7E2` | alternate flat backgrounds |
| ink | `#151312` | text, outlines (NOT used as a background — owner: no black backgrounds) |
| marigold | `#FFB224` | object fills, callout rings, highlights (never as text on paper) |
| ember | `#C2410C` | accent text on light backgrounds |
| stone | `#8C847C` | secondary text, ≥ 30 px at 720 w |
| wreck | `#E5484D` | THE CATCH + WRECK only |
One accent per scene; accent ≤ 15 % of the frame. Why: warm hues read as excitement (Labrecque & Milne 2012);
red kept for emotional beats so it keeps its punch (Kuniecki 2015).
Font: Inter Tight (800–900 headlines, tabular numbers).

## Logo v5 "Falling letter" — LOCKED (live on the channel)
- Wordmark: lowercase "spec or wreck", Inter Tight 900, tracking −4 %; "or" in ember; final **k tips over** 18° from
  its baseline corner, dropped 4 px. Avatar: "s" upright ink + "w" tipped 18° in wreck red.
- Research: descriptive logo (Luffarelli 2019), implied motion (Cian 2014), asymmetry ↔ excitement, moderately
  elaborate (Henderson & Cote 1998), round + one angular break (Jiang 2016).
- Video end card: k snaps upright with a click (SPEC) or falls further with a thud (WRECK).
