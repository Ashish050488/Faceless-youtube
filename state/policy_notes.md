# YouTube policy notes (read 2026-11-16; re-check monthly)

Sources:
- Channel monetization policies (updated 2025-07-15): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- Altered/synthetic content disclosure: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- YPP eligibility: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851

## Inauthentic content (biggest risk for us)
- NOT ok: "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production"
- NOT ok: "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative"
- NOT ok: same storyline template with only the topic swapped
- OK: same intro/outro if the bulk differs; AI for scripts/visuals if the result has creative or educational value
- => every video needs a real narrative arc, a take, varied structure, varied visuals.

## Reused content
- NOT ok: footage uploaded by others, clips compiled from social media, just reading text we didn't write
- Applies even WITH the original creator's permission; penalty is the whole channel.

## Unsatisfying content
- NOT ok: no clear narrative arc; unrelated AI clips stitched for shock.

## AI personas on sensitive topics
- NOT ok: an AI persona presenting as an expert on health, legal, FINANCIAL or political topics
  ("AI-generated podcast hosts offering financial guidance"). => avoid finance-advice niches.

## Disclosure (AI use setting in Studio / containsSyntheticMedia)
- Required: realistic AI scenes that didn't happen, altered real footage/places, real person saying things they didn't.
- Not required: AI scripts, titles, thumbnails, AI voice over non-realistic visuals, animated/diagram content, captions.
- Disclosing does not hurt reach or monetisation; not disclosing can.

## YPP thresholds
- 1,000 subs AND (4,000 public long-form watch hours / 12 months OR 10M Shorts views / 90 days)
- 2-Step Verification on, AdSense linked, no strikes. Review ~1 month, whole channel checked by humans.
- New YPP terms must be accepted in Studio by 2027-01-31.
