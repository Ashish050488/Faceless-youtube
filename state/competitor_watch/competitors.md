## What it shows
- Tags don't drive reach: Neural Stack has zero tags and a 3.1M video; Venom uses the same 7 tags everywhere. Keep
  Techy-style product tags (cheap, helps search), but titles + retention are what matter.
- Titles that win: big brand + one surprising claim/number + curiosity, often one CAPS word and one emoji
  ("NVIDIA Built a Supercomputer With NO Wires?! 🤯", "World's LARGEST Graphics Card! 😱").
- Length: the fastest-growing faceless EN channel runs ~43 s; Techy 75–90 s; ours 82 s.
- Frequency: fast growers post ~2/day (we start 2/day on 18 Nov).
- Topics with the biggest hits: NVIDIA/GPUs, Apple, AI robots, "world's biggest/smallest", price shocks.
