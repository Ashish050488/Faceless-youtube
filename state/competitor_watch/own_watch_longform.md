## What we do instead in long videos
1. Open on the stakes/consequence for the viewer (e.g. "An AI that can spend your money — here's what stops it").
2. Tease the catch in the first 15 s and pay it off at ~60–70 % runtime, not before.
3. Never hold one diagram > 6 s without a new element; never quote paragraphs on screen (max 6 words, big type).
4. Every concept gets a real-world consumer angle (which product, what it costs, should you care).
5. Structure as questions the viewer has, not as the press release's order.
6. Chapter changes = colour/energy/music change.
7. End with a verdict + an end-screen bridge to a related video. Pinned question.
