### Long-form lessons (all 4 of their long videos flopped, 643–2,962 views)
Pacing was NOT the main problem (Sigma cut every ~3 s and still flopped). Common causes:
1. Hook states news/feature, not stakes or conflict.
2. The tension (risk, catch, flaw) is buried after the midpoint and never teased early.
3. Spec-sheet / feature-matrix middle with no question being answered.
4. No comparison to rivals the viewer knows; no story/persona.
5. Same flat voice and music bed for the whole video; no chapter energy changes.
6. No CTA, no end-screen bridge.
7. Shorts-trained audience; long-form needs its own search/browse topics.
