# sow005 — Apple HomeView teardown — Short script (~120 words, target 50–58 s)
Facts: factsheet.md. Numbers as words for the voice.

[HOOK]
Apple made a two hundred ninety-nine dollar smart display.
And iFixit could barely get inside it.

[REVEAL]
It's the new HomeView: a seven-inch screen and an A eighteen chip.

[GLUE]
iFixit says the screen glue held on harder than on any Apple product they've opened recently.

[DAMAGE]
Getting in, they ripped a connector... and snapped a speaker in two.

[HINGE]
The screen's cables run through the hinge, behind locked screws.
So the two halves can't come apart without special tools.

[WOOFER]
The woofer cable is so short, it's hard to plug back in.

[GOOD NEWS]
The good news? The USB-C port is modular, and easy to reach.

[CONTEXT]
That's a step backward. Apple's newer iPads and HomePods got easier to fix.

[VERDICT]
So, to repair? Wreck.

[LOOP]
Would you buy a gadget you can't fix? Spec or wreck?
